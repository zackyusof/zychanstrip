01 / CHARACTER

Three colour engines.

ALGO uses purpose-built waveshaping. NAM runs embedded hardware captures. CLEAN removes the added colour while keeping EQ, manual gain, compression and limiting.

02 / TIME

One timing contract.

At 48 kHz, the complete rack reports 247 samples (5.146 ms). All modes, module orders, bypass states and mix values keep that same latency at this host rate.

03 / LEVEL

Two different matchers.

Auto Gain compares uncorrected rack output with aligned input before master and mix. UNITY compares the completed post-master, post-mix output with that input. Both apply stereo-linked correction.

Where the ceiling ends. Enhance limits at its position in the rack. Enabled rack Auto Gain adds a ceiling-aware safety stage after the complete rack. Master, dry/wet mix and UNITY follow both; the Enhance ceiling is therefore not a guaranteed final-output ceiling.
